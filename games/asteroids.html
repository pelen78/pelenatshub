Flight tips & game rules

Direct controls: arrows or WASD move in screen directions, including diagonals. The ship turns to aim in your last direction. Hold Space to fire continuously.

Classic controls: ← → / A D turn, ↑ / W accelerates, ↓ / S brakes. Releasing thrust slows the ship gradually. Keep moving and shooting together.

You wrap around screen edges. A visible shield protects you after a crash. Every 5,000 points earns an extra life. Records are separate for Direct and Classic; your old record remains in Classic.
