      Dino Run: game guide

      Goal

      Run as far as you can without hitting anything. Jump ground obstacles, duck under low-flying birds, and rack up points as the course speeds up. One hit ends the run.

      Controls

      • Space, ↑ or W jumps.
      • Hold ↓ or S to duck under orange birds; release to stand back up.
      • P pauses. On touch devices, use the on-screen Jump and Hold to duck buttons.

      Modes and scoring

      Score grows with distance run, plus 10 bonus points for each bird you correctly duck under. Daily Challenge gives everyone the exact same obstacle sequence for the day, so scores are fair to compare; Free play generates a new random trail every run. The "Your next goal" panel tracks a running target (like ducking a set number of birds), and the local leaderboard keeps the top 10 scores saved on this computer, separately for each mode.
