Space Invaders: game guide

Goal

Defend Earth from wave after wave of invaders. Clear a formation to advance to the next wave, and face a boss mothership every fifth wave. Losing all three ships, or letting the invaders reach the ground, ends the run.

Controls

  • ← → or A D move your ship.
  • Hold Space to fire, P pauses.
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen left/right buttons and the Fire button.

Scoring and waves

Invaders are worth 10 to 30 points depending on type; armored invaders (from wave 3 on) take two hits. Use the shelters for cover, but your own shots can wear them down too. Every 1,500 points earns an extra life, and a red bonus ship occasionally crosses the top of the screen for extra points. Every fifth wave is a boss mothership: keep firing at its hull until its health bar empties.

Goals and fleet

The "Next goal" panel tracks a running challenge, like defeating a number of invaders or catching the bonus ship, which unlocks a new ship, shot or sky look. Open Your fleet to see what is unlocked; every look has the same speed, hitbox and firepower.
