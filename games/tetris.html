Tetris: game guide

Goal

Guide falling pieces into place to fill horizontal lines, which clear and score points. The board fills up if lines pile too high; lock a piece above the visible playfield and the game ends.

Controls

  • ← → or A D move. ↓ soft drops (faster fall).
  • ↑ or X rotates clockwise; Z rotates counterclockwise.
  • Space drops the piece instantly. C or Shift holds a piece once per turn.
  • P or Esc pauses. The outline on the board shows where the piece will land.

Modes and scoring

Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 lines at once scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 points times your level; back-to-back Tetrises (4 lines) and consecutive clears earn bonus points. The level rises every 10 lines, which speeds up the fall. Marathon sets a line goal from your starting level; Sprint races the clock to clear 40 lines; Ultra scores as much as possible in 90 seconds. Sprint and Ultra always start at level 1.

Goals and blocks

Clearing 5 lines in one run unlocks the Glass block design, and clearing a Tetris (4 lines at once) unlocks Blueprint. Open Your blocks to see what is unlocked and equip a different look; every design plays exactly the same.
