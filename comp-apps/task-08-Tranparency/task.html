Your Mission

In this lesson, you will learn how to select just one part of an image in GIMP. Once you can select, you can delete a background or change its color without touching the rest of the picture. Your Toolkit

These are the four tools you will use. Find the selection tools in the Toolbox or press their keyboard shortcut.

Tool · Shortcut U

Fuzzy Select

Also called the magic wand. Click one spot and it selects all the touching pixels with a similar color. Best for backgrounds with one solid color.

  • Shift + click adds more areas.
  • Threshold in Tool Options: higher selects more, lower selects less.

Tool · Shortcut F

Free Select (Lasso)

You draw the selection yourself, point by point, around the object. Best when the background has many colors.

  • Click to add points around the edge.
  • Click the first point to close the shape, then press Enter.
  • Backspace deletes the last point.

Menu · Ctrl + I

Invert

Flips your selection: what was selected is now unselected, and the other way around. Select the object, invert, and now you have the background.

  • Select › Invert

Menu

Desaturate

Takes the color out of the selected area and makes it black and white.

  • Colors › Desaturate › Color to Gray

Zoom in for the details. Press + and - to zoom while you select. Made a mistake? Ctrl + Z undoes it. When you finish, go to Select › None so the selection doesn't follow you to the next step.

The Exercises

Do the exercises in order. Every exercise ends the same way: export the finished image into your Desktop › GIMP › Transparency folder.

Exercise 01 · Fuzzy Select

Remove the background

File: Bob
  1. Open the file and go to Layer › Transparency › Add Alpha Channel.
  2. Pick the Fuzzy Select Tool.
  3. Click the background.
  4. Go to Edit › Cut. The background turns into a checkerboard.
  5. Go to Select › None and export it as bob.png.

Exercise 02 · Free Select + Black and White

Black and white background

File: Lotus
  1. Open the file.
  2. Pick the Free Select Tool.
  3. Zoom in and click small points all around the edge of the lotus. Take your time around the petals.
  4. Click your first point to close the shape, then press Enter.
  5. Go to Select › Invert. Now the background is selected, not the flower.
  6. Go to Colors › Desaturate › Color to Gray and click OK.
  7. Go to Select › None and export it as lotus.jpg.

Think about it

Why .jpg this time?

You didn't delete anything in this image, so nothing is transparent. You only changed colors. A JPG works fine here, and the file is smaller.

Exercise 03 · Free Select + Transparent background

Same steps for all four files: RF, Soccer, Basket and Ski.

  1. Open the file and go to Layer › Transparency › Add Alpha Channel.
  2. Pick the Free Select Tool and click points around the person. Include what they are using, like a ball, a racket or skis.
  3. Click your first point to close the shape, then press Enter.
  4. Go to Select › Invert.
  5. Press Delete. Everything except the person turns into a checkerboard.
  6. Go to Select › None and export it. Decide which format is the right one for this image: .png or .jpg?
