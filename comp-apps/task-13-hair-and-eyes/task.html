Your Mission

In this assignment, you will keep practicing layers and selection tools. You will also learn new tools: Blend Modes and Layer Opacity (transparency). You will change the hair color and the eye color of 7 pictures, then add them to the presentation you made for Healing & Clone.

You will learn · 01

Paint on a new layer

Add a transparent layer and paint the new color there, never on the photo.

You will learn · 02

Blend Modes

Change how your paint mixes with the picture, so the color looks real.

You will learn · 03

Layer Opacity

Make your color more see-through until it looks natural.

Your Toolkit

You only need four things, and three of them live in the Layers panel.

Layers panel · Right-click

New Layer

Right-click the Background layer › New Layer. Fill it with Transparency. You paint here, never on the photo.

Tool · Shortcut P

Paintbrush

Paints the new color on your layer. Fix the edges with the Eraser Shift + E.

Layers panel · Mode

Blend Mode

Decides how your paint mixes with the picture below. Overlay keeps the details of the eye and the hair.

Layers panel · Opacity

Opacity

This is layer transparency. Lower it to make the new color softer and more natural.

How it works · Try it

Pick the eye or the hair and click each step. Then play with the Mode, the Opacity and the colors in the Layers panel.
