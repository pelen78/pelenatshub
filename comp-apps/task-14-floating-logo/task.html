Your Mission

Create two versions of a floating text logo in GIMP. First, learn the technique by following a detailed tutorial. Then, design a version with your own images and textures.

Part 01

Follow the tutorial

Build a 3D text logo with layers, filters, a layer mask, Bump Map and a shadow. Use a solid-color background.

Part 02

Create your own

Design a second version with a custom texture inside the letters and an image background.

Before you start · Your folder
  1. Open File Explorer. On your Desktop, open your GIMP folder.
  2. Inside it, create a folder called FloatingLogo. Write it exactly like that: capital F, capital L, no spaces.
  3. Save everything in this folder: your XCF files, your JPG and PNG exports, and the images you download for Part 02.
Part 01 · Follow the Tutorial

Choose your name or a short word with 5 letters max. Use a thick, bold font so the texture and 3D effect are easy to see. Download the PDF below and carefully follow the GIMP tutorial. Your finished version must have a solid-color background.

Tutorial · PDF · Pat David GIMP: Simple Floating Logo PDF on Google Drive ↓ Download the tutorial

When you finish, export two versions of your logo: a JPG with your solid-color background, and a transparent PNG with only your letters and shadow.

Part 02 · Make It Yours

Now create a second version of your text logo using your own images. Apply the GIMP techniques you learned to produce a new look.

Image 01

Letter texture

Download a picture or pattern and use it to fill the inside of your letters.

Image 02

Background image

Download another picture for the background behind your logo.

  • Your design keeps a clear floating or 3D effect.
  • Your letters are easy to read.
  • Your background works well with the texture. If the letters get lost, try a different picture.
Turning it in

For each part, save your editable GIMP project and export two versions: one with a background and one without. You must submit six files total.

Understand the formats

JPG

With background

Your complete logo with its visible background. JPG does not support transparency.

PNG

Transparent

Your logo and its shadow, with the background hidden before export.

XCF

Editable GIMP file

The original project with all your working layers and masks.
