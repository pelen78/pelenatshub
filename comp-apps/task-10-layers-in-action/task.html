Your Mission

Create an original layered scene in GIMP on a topic of your choice. Find at least five original images on the Internet: one for your background and at least four more to add to your scene. You must remove the background from each of those four images yourself in GIMP and combine everything using separate layers.

While you work, take real screenshots of your editing process. At the end, create a Google Slides presentation explaining your editing process, and submit your finished GIMP files.

Check-In · Getting Ready

Before opening GIMP, organize your project and collect the images you want to use.

  1. Decide what kind of scene you want to create. Think about where it takes place and which four or more objects, people or animals you want to add.
  2. On your computer's Desktop, open your GIMP folder. Inside it, create a new folder called LayersInAction.
  3. Search the Internet and download at least 5 original images into this folder: one background and four or more foreground images. Keep the images as they were downloaded.
  4. Give each image a clear name (for example, background.jpg, person.jpg or dog.jpg).
  5. Open GIMP. Remember to take screenshots as you work: you will need them for your presentation.

Your Toolkit · Layers

Think of layers as clear sheets stacked on top of one another. Each object sits on its own sheet, so you can move or resize it without changing the other images.

Why separate layers?

  • You can select one object without changing the others.
  • You can scale, move and flip individual elements.
  • You can change their order to put one object in front of or behind another.
  • You can show or hide a layer using the eye icon.

Remember: The background stays at the bottom. Your four or more cutouts go above it.

Important menu

Open as Layers

File › Open as Layers adds another image to the same GIMP project instead of opening it in a different document.

Important menu

Add Alpha Channel

Layer › Transparency › Add Alpha Channel allows the background of a layer to become transparent.

Selection tools

Free Select or Fuzzy Select

Use Free Select (Lasso) for detailed shapes, or Fuzzy Select for a simple, solid-color background.

Transformation tools

Move, Scale & Order

Select the correct layer in the Layers Panel before you move it, change its size or place it above or below another layer.

Project Rules
  • Downloading images with their backgrounds already removed (transparent cutouts, stickers or isolated PNGs).
  • Using online background removers, AI tools or another editor to do the cutting for you.
  • Turning in only a flattened final picture without the editable layers.
