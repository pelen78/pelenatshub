Your Mission

In this lesson, you will learn how to use the Healing Tool and the Clone Tool in GIMP. You will edit each picture to remove imperfections such as wrinkles, scars, blemishes or unwanted objects. After editing your images, you will create a presentation in Google Slides to show your before and after results.

You will learn · 01

Healing Tool

Blend away small spots, wrinkles and scars so they match the skin around them.

You will learn · 02

Clone Tool

Copy one part of a picture and paint it over something you want to hide.

Your Toolkit

Both tools live together in the Toolbox, under the rubber stamp icon. The fastest way to pick them is the keyboard shortcut. Both work the same way: first you choose a source (the good area), then you paint over the problem.

Tool · Shortcut H

Healing Tool

Copies the texture from a clean area and blends it with the colors around the spot, so the fix looks natural. Best for skin.

Tool · Shortcut C

Clone Tool

Copies the pixels exactly from one place to another, like a stamp. Best for hiding objects.

Clone vs Healing

Clone ToolHealing Tool
What it doesCopies pixels exactly, color and all.Copies texture, then blends it with the colors around the spot.
ResultAn exact copy. You may see edges if the colors don't match.A soft fix that blends in.
Best forHiding objects: a person in the background, a sign, a wire.Skin: wrinkles, scars, blemishes, small spots.

Step 1 · Download your images

Organize your files before you open GIMP. All your images go in one folder.

  1. Download all the images attached to this assignment in Google Classroom.
  2. Open File Explorer. On your Desktop, open your GIMP folder. Inside it, create a folder called Retouch. Write it exactly like that: capital R, no spaces.
  3. Move the images you downloaded into your Retouch folder.
  4. Open GIMP and open your first image with File › Open.
