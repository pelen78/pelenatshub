Your Mission

In this assignment, you will make a collage in GIMP. You will fill a shape with at least 40 pictures of something you like, and every picture goes on its own layer. At the end, you will submit a Google Drive link to a folder with your collage as a .jpg and your GIMP file as an .xcf.

You will learn · 01

Layers

Each picture lives on its own layer, so you can change it without touching the others.

You will learn · 02

Move, Scale, Rotate

Change the position, size and angle of every picture until the shape is full.

You will learn · 03

.XCF vs .JPG

The .xcf keeps all your layers so you can keep editing. The .jpg is the finished picture anyone can open.

Choose Your Shape

Pick one of these four shapes. You will find them attached to this assignment in Google Classroom. Download only the one you choose.

Check-In · Getting ready

Organize your files before you open GIMP. All your images go in one folder.

  1. Choose your topic. Pick something you like: a sport, a team, a band, a video game, food, animals or cars. All your pictures must be about that one topic.
  2. Open File Explorer. On your Desktop, open your GIMP folder. Inside it, create a folder called Collage.
  3. Download your shape from Google Classroom and move it into your Collage folder.
  4. Search for pictures of your topic. Click each picture to open it bigger, then right-click it, choose Save image as… and save it in your Collage folder.
  5. Repeat until you have at least 40 pictures. Check in File Explorer: Desktop › GIMP › Collage

Big pictures, not thumbnails. Small pictures look blurry when you make them bigger. Always open the picture before you save it.

Build Your Collage
  1. Open your shape in GIMP.
  2. Delete the inside of the shape and fill the outside with a color.
  3. Add every picture you downloaded with File › Open as Layers.
  4. In the Layers panel, move every picture layer below the shape layer.
  5. Move, scale and rotate each picture until the shape is full and looks like a collage.
Turning it in

Save your project as Lastname_Collage.xcf and export it as Lastname_Collage.jpg in your Collage folder. Then submit one link in Google Classroom:

  1. Upload your Collage folder from your computer into your GIMP folder in Google Drive.
  2. Check that the folder has your .xcf and your .jpg.
  3. Copy the link to your Collage folder and submit it in Google Classroom.

Check your link before you turn in. A link I can't open counts as not turned in.
