ROOM LB6 · SCHOOL YEAR 2026–2027

Project Goal

You are going to create a screen-recorded video tutorial that proves you know how to use an important Google tool.

Your tutorial must clearly show every step on the screen while you explain what you are doing using subtitles, not your voice.

Your assigned tool: I will assign you one of the six Google tools below. You only need to complete that one section. Check with me if you're not sure which one is yours.
General Video Requirements

These rules apply no matter which tool you were assigned.

  • Be a screen recording.
  • Be at least 3 minutes long.
  • Be saved as a video file before uploading it to YouTube.
  • Use subtitles to explain every step.
  • Have NO voice narration.
  • Music is optional.
  • Be uploaded to YouTube using your personal Gmail account.
  • Be submitted as an UNLISTED YouTube video.
Before you record: close personal tabs and make sure you do not show passwords, private messages, or other personal information.
Your Tool

Open only the section your teacher assigned you. Your video needs to show and explain everything listed inside it.

1YouTube

Create a tutorial showing how to upload and manage a video on YouTube.

  • How to upload a video to YouTube.
  • How to add a title and description.
  • The difference between these visibility options:
    • Public
    • Private
    • Unlisted
    • Scheduled
  • When each visibility option should be used.
  • How to add and edit subtitles.
  • How to copy and share the video link.
Your final tutorial video must be set to UNLISTED.
2Gmail

Create a tutorial showing how to use and organize Gmail.

  • How to compose and send an email.
  • How to correctly use:
    • To
    • CC
    • BCC
    • Subject
  • How to attach a file to an email.
  • How to create and use labels.
  • What labels are used for.
  • How to create a contact group.
  • How to send an email to a group.
  • How to create an email signature with:
    • Text
    • An image
3Google Forms

Create a sample survey while explaining how Google Forms works.

  • How to create a new Google Form.
  • How to add different types of questions.
  • When to use different question types, such as:
    • Short answer
    • Paragraph
    • Multiple choice
    • Checkboxes
    • Dropdown
    • Linear scale
    • Date
    • Time
  • How to duplicate a question.
  • How to make a question Required.
  • How to change important Form settings.
  • How to preview your form.
  • How to publish and share your form.
  • The difference between sharing the form with someone who will answer it and someone who can edit it.
  • How to view responses.
  • How to send responses to a Google Sheet.

Create a sample Google Site. Your site must include 3 main pages and at least 2 subpages.

  • How to create a Google Site.

Create and organize files using Google Drive. Start by creating a main folder using your name.

Before You Submit
  • My video is at least 3 minutes long.
  • I demonstrated all the required skills for my assigned Google tool.
  • I explained the steps using subtitles.
  • I did not use voice narration.
  • My YouTube video is set to UNLISTED.
  • I tested the video link before submitting it.
Turn In
  1. Upload your finished video to YouTube, set to Unlisted.
  2. Test your own link in a private window before submitting it.
  3. Open Google Classroom and find this assignment.
  4. Click Add or create → Link, paste your YouTube video link, and click Add.
  5. Click Turn in.
Submit your YouTube video link in Google Classroom. A link that doesn't open, or a video that isn't Unlisted, counts as not turned in.
