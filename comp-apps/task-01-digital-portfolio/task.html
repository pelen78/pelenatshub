Your Mission

This year, you will build your own academic website. It starts as a way to introduce yourself and it becomes the place where you collect your best work from all your classes, all year long.

You will make your page in Wix. You start with an empty page. Nobody gives you a design. You make it. You choose how it looks: the colors, the fonts, and where things go. When you finish, you publish it. Then you get a real link you can share with other people.

Why an empty page? A template already made every choice for you. This project is about your choices.

Rule: AI is only allowed to make images for your page. Nothing else. he design, the pages and the words are yours.

Check-In · Setting up Wix

Do these three steps on your own. We'll build the rest together in class.

  1. Go to wix.com and sign up with your school email.
  2. Open the template gallery and choose Blank Template. Do not pick a designed template. You should land in the Editor with an empty page, a header and a footer.

Mobile matters. Before you publish, click the mobile view icon in the Editor and fix anything that overlaps, overflows, or becomes unreadable. Half of the people who visit your site will open it on a phone.

Site Structure

Your passport needs three pages in your Wix Pages menu, plus four subpages inside Travel Log. Every page must be easy to find from the menu.

Section 01

Home

  • Your name and grade
  • An appropriate avatar (see below)
  • Your interests
  • Three things about you
  • One learning goal for this year

Section 02

My Tech Story

  • A short timeline of you and technology
  • The 5 moments (see below)
  • At least one image per moment

Section 03

Travel Log

  • One Wix subpage per grade (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th)
  • Inside each grade: a section for every subject
  • Ready for projects: empty for now, but organized

Passport Photo · Avatar and images

Every passport needs a photo. On your Home page you need an avatar that represents you.

This is the exception to the no-AI rule: images. You may use ChatGPT for your avatar and for any other image your site needs. Everything else: your text, your layout, your design decisions, is still 100% yours.

  1. Use a picture of yourself that you already have. Only you in the picture, never a classmate or a family member.
  2. Open ChatGPT, upload your photo and paste the prompt below. Change the parts in brackets so the avatar looks like you.
  3. Ask for changes until you like it: "make the background yellow", "add my glasses", "make it more cartoon". That's part of the exercise.
  4. Download the image and add it to your Wix site as your avatar.

Example prompt · copy and edit

Turn this photo of me into a friendly flat illustration avatar for my school portfolio website. Keep my [hair, skin tone, glasses] so it still looks like me. Simple vector style, bold flat colors, plain [color] background, head and shoulders only, no text, square image.

Don't want to use your photo? That's completely fine. Describe yourself to ChatGPT instead, or design your own avatar in Wix with shapes and icons. What matters is that it is appropriate, that it represents you, and that it looks good next to the rest of your Home page.

Other images for your site

You can also use ChatGPT to create any other image your site needs: a banner for your Home page, an illustration for a Tech Story moment when you don't have a real photo, icons for your subjects, or a background for a section. Ask for the image, download it, and add it in Wix.

Example prompt · images

Create a horizontal banner image for my school portfolio website about [soccer and video editing]. Flat illustration style, bold colors that match [purple, coral and yellow], plenty of empty space so I can put text on top, no words in the image.

Two things to keep in mind. First, ask for the same style every time: a site where every image looks different looks unfinished. Second, a real photo of your own is always better than a generated one when you actually have it: your Tech Story is your story.

Turning it in

You turn in a link, not a file. Nothing gets emailed, nothing gets downloaded.

  1. In Wix, click Publish. Nobody can see your site until you do this.
  2. Copy your link. It looks like yourname.wixsite.com/…
  3. Open Google Classroom and find this assignment, Digital Passport.
  4. Click Add or create → Link, paste your Wix link, and click Add.
  5. Click Turn in. If you don't press Turn in, it isn't submitted.

Test your own link first. Open it in a private window, or send it to yourself. If it asks for a password or says the page is private, go back to Wix and publish it properly. A link I can't open counts as not turned in.

Border Control · Rules
  • No AI for your text, your design, or your pages. Images are the only exception: see Passport Photo.
  • No Wix AI site builder. Start with an empty page and build it yourself.
  • No photos of other people in AI tools. Your avatar comes from a photo of you, alone.
  • No ready-made templates. You start with an empty page. That is the point of the project.
  • No leftover default content. If it still says "Click to edit", it isn't finished.
  • No private or sensitive information (address, phone, personal details).
  • No paid plans. The free Wix plan is all you need: never enter payment information.
  • Wix sections, icons, shapes, colors, fonts, and appropriate images are allowed.
  • You make your own design and organization decisions.
  • Your site must have clear navigation and a working published link.
Usability Test

Before your passport gets stamped as complete, a classmate will visit your site. They have two minutes to find:

→ What does this person want to learn this year?

→ What is one of their interests?

→ Where would you put a Science project from this year?

The rule: if your classmate can't find it quickly, you improve your navigation, headings, or organization. Good design doesn't just look good; it's easy to use.
