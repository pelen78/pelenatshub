GIMP 3 · Image editing

A layer is one slice of the burger.

In GIMP, each part of a picture can live on its own layer. Layers stack like the parts of a cheeseburger: whatever sits higher in the stack covers whatever is under it.

Part 1

Play with the layers

This works like the Layers panel in GIMP. Click a layer to select it, drag a row to change the order, and click the eye to hide it. Switch between Exploded (how the layers stack) and Flat (what GIMP shows you).

In Flat view, drag on the picture to move the selected layers.

Each layer is a clear sheet the size of the canvas. Stack them and you get the burger.
