Your Mission

In this lesson, you will learn what a layer is and why every part of a picture should live on its own layer. You will practice in the Burger Layers Lab, build a cheeseburger in GIMP with one part on each layer, and answer a short quiz. At the end, you will answer the Google Forms quiz, with your personal Lab code in the first question, and turn in one link in Google Classroom: the link to your Layers folder in Google Drive.

You will learn

  • What a layer is
  • The order of the layers and the eye feature
  • .XCF vs .PNG

Everything you need is in the Burger Layers Lab: the practice, the step-by-step guide for GIMP and a review video. The link is also in Google Classroom.

Check-In · Getting ready

Do these steps before you start. Organized files now means no lost work later.

  1. On your computer's Desktop, open your GIMP folder. If you don't have it, create a folder called GIMP.
  2. Inside it, create a subfolder called Layers. Write it exactly like that: capital L, no spaces. Your burger files go here.
  3. Open the Burger Layers Lab in your browser.
  4. Open GIMP. Put the Lab and GIMP side by side so you can follow the steps while you work.
Your Toolkit

These are the tools you will use. Find them in the Toolbox or press their keyboard shortcut.

Panel · Right side

Layers panel

Every layer in your image is listed here. The layer at the top of the list is the front of the picture.

  • The eye hides or shows a layer.
  • Drag a layer up or down to change the order.
  • Double-click a name to rename it.

Tool · Shortcut E

Ellipse Select

Selects a circle or an oval. You will use it for the bun.

  • Type Position and Size in Tool Options for an exact circle.
  • Hold Shift while you drag to keep it round.

Tool · Shortcut R

Rectangle Select

Selects a square or a rectangle. You will use it for the meat and the cheese.

  • Same Position and Size fields as Ellipse Select.

Tool · Shortcut F

Free Select (Lasso)

You draw the selection yourself. You will use it for the lettuce.

  • Click and drag a wavy line around the bun.
  • End on your first point, then press Enter.

Tool · Shortcut Shift + B

Bucket Fill

Fills your selection with the foreground color.

  • In Tool Options, set Affected area to Fill whole selection.

Tool · Shortcut Shift + R

Rotate

Turns the selected layer. You will use it to turn the cheese.

  • Click the canvas, type Angle 45 and click Rotate.
