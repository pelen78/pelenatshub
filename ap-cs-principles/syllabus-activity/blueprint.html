Why start here

Most AP courses spend day one on rules. You don't need that. What you do need is a clear picture of what you signed up for.

This course ends in a real exam, written by the College Board, scored on numbers that are already public. Seventy multiple-choice questions. A performance task you build yourself. A fixed number of minutes for each. None of that is a surprise you find out in April; it's all in the syllabus, today.

So: read it and answer seven questions. Everything is in the document. Find it, don't guess.
