What You Will Learn

Throughout Unit 1, you will learn how programming is used to create computational artifacts and solve problems. You will also practice designing algorithms, finding errors, comparing possible solutions, and thinking about how programmers work with others.

01

Programs & Algorithms

  • Know what programs and algorithms are.
  • Understand how a sequence of instructions can solve a problem.
02

Precise Instructions

  • Understand why computers need instructions that are clear and exact.
  • Recognize why small changes in instructions can change a program's result.
03

Debugging

  • Be able to spot errors in a program.
  • Practice fixing bugs and testing whether a solution works.
04

Problem-Solving & Creativity

  • See programming as a tool for problem-solving and creativity.
  • Compare different ways to solve the same computational problem.
