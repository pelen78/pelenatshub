Team Generator

Create randomized student teams in seconds. Adjust the teams by hand before you share them with your class.

Class and names

Pick a class, then paste or type its student names.

One name per line, or paste a column from Google Sheets. Saved automatically in this browser.

You can also drag a .txt or .pdf file onto the box. PDFs must contain real text (not a scanned photo). Check the list after loading.

Make teams

Choose how to split the class.

Teams will be as evenly sized as possible.

Names and classes are stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent online or synced between devices.

Your teams

Generate teams to see them here.

Ready when you are.Add names, choose a team size, and press Generate teams.

Need to change something? Use "Move to..." next to a student. Moving students by hand can make team sizes uneven.
