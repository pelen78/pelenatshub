What's This About?

This week you're not building anything yet. You're exploring your playground. You'll watch maker projects, tour the space, and keep a Google Doc where you record ideas and inspiration as you go. By Friday, you'll use everything you collected to build your own Maker Profile.

Your Week

Monday
  • Watch maker projects in youtube
  • Record at least 5 that catch your attention in a Google Doc: what it is and why it interests you
  • Mark your favorite and say why
  • Assignment walkthrough
Tuesday
  • Tools and reources around the classroom: 3D printer, Arduino, design software
  • Add what you discover to your Inspiration Doc
Wednesday
  • Build your Maker Profile
  • Include all 6 required elements. You can use Googel Slides, Capcut, Canva or any other tool to make your profile
  • Submit to Google Classroom
Thursday
  • Show your profile
  • Vote for most creative dream project
  • What surprised you most about this space, and what are you most excited to learn this year?

Your Maker Profile Must Include These 6 Elements

All five are required. Everything else is your creative choice.

01
Your Name Something that makes it yours: play with fonts, colors, layout.
02
3 Words That Describe You as a Maker Think: curious, builder, problem-solver...
03
Your Dream Project If you had all the tools and time, what would you build?
04
The Unit You're Most Excited About Arduino, 3D Design, Fusion 360, Blender, or Java: pick one and say why.
05
At Least One Image A photo of yourself, something you've made, or inspiration for your dream project.
06
Your Inspiration Doc List at least 5 maker projects that caught your attention. For each one, write what it is and why it interests you. Mark which one is your favorite and explain why.

Grading Criteria

Element What We're Looking For
All 6 Elements Present Name, 3 words, dream project, favorite unit, at least one image, and your Inspiration Doc.
Effort & Creativity The profile feels personal and intentional — not rushed or generic.
Participation Actively engaged during the tour, demos, and exploration days.
