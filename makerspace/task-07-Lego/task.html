Your Mission

Today you will make your first design in Fusion 360. First, you will follow a video to build a 2x4 toy block. Then you will use the Timeline to turn it into a block of a different size, without starting over.

You will learn · 01

Sketch & Extrude

Draw a 2D sketch with exact dimensions and turn it into a 3D body.

You will learn · 02

Pattern, Shell, Fillet

Copy the studs, hollow out the block and round its edges.

You will learn · 03

The Timeline

Go back to any step of your design, change it, and let Fusion rebuild the rest.

Part 01 · Build the 2x4 Block

Watch the video and build the block in Fusion 360 at the same time. Pause it whenever you need to.

  1. Set your units to millimeters, like the video shows.
  2. Follow the video until your 2x4 block is finished.
  3. Save your design as Lastname_Block2x4.
Part 02 · Challenge

Now turn your block into a new size 2x2 (15.8 mm) using the Timeline.

Part 03 · Cheat Sheet

Open the cheat sheet template and go to File › Make a copy. Name your copy Lastname_Fusion_CheatSheet.

For each of these 6 tools, write what it does in one sentence, where you find it in Fusion, and add a screenshot from your own block.
