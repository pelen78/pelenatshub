Your Mission

In this assignment, you will get started with Autodesk Fusion 360, a 3D design program, and learn to recognize 10 important parts of its user interface.

Register for educational access using your ATS school account, install Fusion 360, watch the interface video, and complete a Google Docs worksheet describing the main function of each interface element. At the end, add one full interface image labeled with numbers 1–10.

Get Fusion 360

To use Fusion 360 for school, register for Autodesk Education access using your ATS account.

  1. Open the Autodesk Education – Students website ↗.
  2. Create an Autodesk account with your ATS school email, or sign in if you already have one. Select the Student education option and complete any eligibility verification Autodesk requests.
  3. Once your educational access is approved, find Fusion, download the application, install it, and sign in with your Autodesk account.
