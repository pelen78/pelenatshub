01

Your turn. Type your initial and make it 8 mm tall. Click Flip so the letter looks backwards on the tray: the ice is a mirror of the tray, so the letter comes out the right way on the cube. Cut the letter 1 mm into the floor with Extrude (Cut). Then use Rectangular Pattern to copy it into all 14 cubes, with the same numbers as before: 7 and 255 mm, 2 and 50 mm. You already know the tools.

Turning it in

Save both designs. Export each one from Fusion with File › Export as .f3d.

Then save each model as an .stl file. In the Browser, right-click Tray › Save As Mesh and choose STL. Do the same with Grip.

Turn in these 4 files in Google Classroom:

  1. Your ice tray with your initial: Lastname_IceTray.f3d
  2. The ice tray as a mesh: Lastname_IceTray.stl
  3. Your grip with your word: Lastname_Grip.f3d
  4. The grip as a mesh: Lastname_Grip.stl

Check your files before you turn in. A file I can't open counts as not turned in.
