Design requirements
Must include
  • Custom image or illustration

  • LED diffusion layer
Technical specs
  • Wall thickness ≥ 2 mm
  • Correct STL export
  • Verified measurements
  • Fits build plate
Project phases
1
Research & inspiration
Look for lightbox ideas online. Download SVG/vector files if needed for your design.
2
Design in Fusion 360
Model the full lightbox: frame, back panel with engraved design, and LED channel. Double-check all measurements.
3
Export & slice STL files
Export each component as STL. Review in slicer software and adjust supports or infill as needed.
4
Print & assemble
Print all parts. Install LED strip, connect wiring, and assemble the final lightbox.
5
Document & present
Photograph your process and final result. Build your slide presentation.
Deliverable — slide presentation
Your presentation must cover:
01
Concept & inspiration — where did your idea come from?
02
Design process in Fusion 360 — screenshots of key steps and dimensions
03
STL files and slicer settings used
04
LED wiring — diagram or photos explaining how you attached the lights
05
Final result — photos of the assembled, lit lightbox
06
Reflection — what worked, what you would change
