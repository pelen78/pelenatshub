Turning it in

Save your design. Export it from Fusion with File › Export as .f3d.

Then save the bottle and the cap as two separate .stl files. In the Browser, right-click Bottle › Save As Mesh and choose STL. Do the same with Cap.

Turn in these 3 files in Google Classroom:

  1. Your bottle with the cap: .f3d
  2. The bottle alone: Lastname_Bottle.stl
  3. The cap alone: Lastname_Cap.stl
