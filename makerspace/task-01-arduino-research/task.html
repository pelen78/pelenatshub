Your Mission

Before you start building with Arduino, you need to understand what it is, why people use it, and what kinds of projects it can control.

For this assignment, you will research Arduino and create a Google Slides presentation with your findings. Your explanations must be written in your own words.

The goal is not to memorize technical details yet. This first task is about understanding the big picture and discovering what Arduino can help you create.

Assignment Overview

What you will do

  1. Research the 8 Arduino topics below.
  2. Find 4 Arduino projects you would like to build.
  3. Choose 1 project that you believe is realistic for you.
  4. Create a Google Slides presentation using your own words.
  5. Create your Arduino account and install the Arduino IDE.
Part 1 · Arduino Research

Your presentation must answer all 8 research sections. Explain the ideas clearly instead of copying definitions.

  • 01What is Arduino?Explain what Arduino is, what it is mainly used for, and what kinds of projects can be created with it.
  • 02Why learn Arduino?Explain at least 3 reasons why learning Arduino can be useful. Think about programming, electronics, problem solving, engineering, robotics, creativity, or building your own projects.
  • 03What is the Arduino IDE?Explain what it is, what it is used for, why it is important, and include 3 important features or characteristics.
  • 04What is an Arduino Sketch?Explain what a Sketch is, what it is used for, and where it is created. You do not need to explain programming commands yet.
  • 05Advantages and limitations of ArduinoResearch and explain 3 advantages and 3 limitations. Do not only list them, briefly explain each one.
  • 06History of ArduinoWho created it? Where and approximately when was it created? Why was Arduino originally developed?
  • 07Arduino in real lifeFind at least 4 different real-life applications. Examples: robotics, automation, medicine, agriculture, engineering, art, education, environmental monitoring, or smart devices.
  • 08Arduino vs. Raspberry PiBriefly compare what each one is, what each is mainly used for, and which projects are better for each. Finish by explaining when you would choose Arduino instead of Raspberry Pi.

Part 3 · Arduino Setup

You will use the Arduino IDE in future class activities, so it must be installed and ready before we begin the practical work.

  1. Visit the official Arduino website.
    Open arduino.cc
  2. Create an Arduino account using your ATS email.
  3. Download and install the Arduino IDE on your computer.
    Arduino IDE download
  4. Open the Arduino IDE to make sure it was installed correctly.

If you have a problem installing the IDE, tell me before the due date. Do not wait until the day we start the practical Arduino activities.

Presentation Requirements

Create your research in Google Slides. Your presentation should be clear, organized, visual, and easy to read.

  • Include your name, date, and a clear title.
  • Include all 8 research sections.
  • Include your 4 Arduino projects and your final project choice.
  • Write all explanations in your own words.
  • Include pictures, diagrams, videos, or a combination of these.
  • Include the sources for the information and media you used.
  • Use a clean, consistent design and text that is easy to read.
  • Do not use the default Google Slides themes.
  • Do not fill slides with long paragraphs.

Design: Create your own design or use a free presentation template from another website. The presentation should look professional, but the design should never make the information difficult to read.

AI Use

You may use AI tools to help you create or design your presentation.

  • You may not use AI to research the answers for you.
  • You may not paste AI-written explanations into your presentation.
  • You may use AI to help with layout, visual design, organization, or presentation creation.
  • Your research, comparisons, explanations, and reflections must show your own understanding.
