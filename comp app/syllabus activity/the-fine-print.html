Why bother

The syllabus is the only document in this class that can cost you credit. Not a project, not an exam, this one.

It says how many absences you get, what happens when you're late, how your grade is split, and what counts as cheating.

So today, before anything else: open the syllabus and work through it on your own. All the answers are in the document. Don't guess and don't ask me, find them.
