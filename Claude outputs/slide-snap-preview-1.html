Turning it in

Export each design from Fusion with File › Export as .f3d. Then right-click the top of the Browser › Save As Mesh and choose STL.

Turn in these 4 files in Google Classroom:

  1. Your Sliding Lid Box: Lastname_SlideBox.f3d
  2. The Sliding Lid Box as a mesh: Lastname_SlideBox.stl
  3. Your Arduino Case: Lastname_ArduinoCase.f3d
  4. The Arduino Case as a mesh: Lastname_ArduinoCase.stl
