This is practice for the real Create Performance Task. Work on your own and make every part of the story yours.
Your Story Must Have
  • At least 4 scenes that tell one story with a beginning, a middle and an end.
  • Graphics in every scene: shapes, text or images that show what is happening.
  • A change between scenes: the program moves to the next scene with a timer or a mouse click.
  • At least one function with parameters that you call more than once, for example to draw a character in different places.
  • Variables to keep track of information, like the current scene.
  • Comments that explain what each part of your code does.
Plan Your Story

Plan your story inside the unit in CodeHS. Complete these lessons carefully, because they are the plan you will follow when you write your code:

01

03

Solving With Pseudocode

  • Write the steps of your program in plain words, scene by scene.
Write it well the first time. Give complete answers in these lessons. A good plan makes the code much easier to write.
