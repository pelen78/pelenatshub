Your Mission

In this lesson, you are going to learn the basics of Wiglo, the animation studio we will use in this unit. First you will set up Wiglo on your computer. Then you will watch a video about its interface.

Step 1 · Get Wiglo

Wiglo lives in a folder on your computer, so you don't need the internet to use it.

  1. Go to the Wiglo folder in Google Drive (the link is in Google Classroom) and download Wiglo para Windows.zip.
  2. Open File Explorer and go to Downloads. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
  3. Move the Wiglo para Windows folder to your Desktop.
  4. Open the folder and double-click Wiglo. Wiglo opens in your browser.
  5. The first time, Wiglo also puts a shortcut on your desktop: a white W on a purple square. From now on, double-click it to open Wiglo.
Important

Do NOT delete the Wiglo para Windows folder. The shortcut needs it. If you move the folder, double-click Wiglo inside it one more time.

Tip: save your .anim projects in your Wiglo para Windows folder too, so everything stays together.
