Your Task

Overview

What are you creating?

Create a short Google Slides presentation that explains the basics of image manipulation.

Your presentation should be easy to understand, visual, and written in your own words.

Slides to Include

Use this outline to organize your presentation.

01

Title Slide

Include the title Image Manipulation, your name, and an image related to photo editing.

02

Pixels

Explain what a pixel is and show an image zoomed in enough to see the pixels.

03

Image Resolution

Explain what image resolution means and show an example of low resolution vs. high resolution.

04

Image Formats

Explain what an image format is and why different formats are used.

05

JPG · PNG · GIF

Compare the three formats. Include their main characteristics and when each one is useful.

06

Image Editing Programs

Choose five programs used to edit images and briefly describe what each one can do.

07

Photoshop vs. GIMP

Compare the two programs. Focus on cost, tools, ease of use, and who might use them.

08

Choose the Best Format

Choose the best format for each situation and explain why:

  • A photograph for a website
  • A logo with a transparent background
  • A short animated image
