Before You Start

Overview

What's this about?

You already know the ideas behind code. Now you write some. In CodeHS you give instructions to a computer, run them, and see what happens.

This is just a first taste. Later in the year we will try another kind of coding.

  1. Go to codehs.com.
  2. Sign up with your school email.
  3. Our class will already be there. Open it to see your lessons.
School email only. If you sign up with a personal email, you will not see our class and your work will not count.
How Each Lesson Works

Every lesson follows the same 3 steps. Do them in order.

01

Watch

Watch the video. Pause it when you need to.

02

Take notes

Write the main ideas in your notes Doc.

03

Do the exercises

Use your notes to solve the exercises.

Stuck on an exercise? Go back to your notes and the video first. The answer is almost always there. Then ask.
Your Notes
