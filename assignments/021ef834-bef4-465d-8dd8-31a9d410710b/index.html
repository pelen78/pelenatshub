Your Mission

A multiplicity picture shows the same person several times in one photo. In this project you will take your own pictures, make a multiplicity picture in GIMP, and create a presentation that explains the procedure step by step.

Step 1 · Take your pictures

A good multiplicity starts with good pictures. Follow these rules when you shoot:

  • Put the camera or phone in one spot and do not move it. Use a tripod or lean it on something.
  • Take one picture for each position of the person.
  • Keep the same light in all the pictures.
  • Don't zoom or change the angle between pictures.
  • Don't let the positions of the person overlap.
  1. Take at least 4 pictures for your project.
  2. Open File Explorer. Inside your GIMP folder, create a folder called Multiplicity.
  3. Save all your pictures in your Multiplicity folder.
Step 2 · Your Presentation

Create a presentation that explains the procedure to make a multiplicity picture. It needs these slides:

  1. 01Title: your name, the project name and the date.
  2. 02Your pictures: at least 4 pictures you took for this project.
  3. 03Materials and requirements for this project.
  4. 04+The steps: explain how to create the multiplicity effect, with screenshots.
  5. ENDFinal product: show your finished multiplicity picture.

Explain, don't mention

Answer HOW?

Be specific. "Select the person" is not enough. Say which tool, where you click and what happens.

Design

Make it yours

Use colorful and creative themes, fonts and images.

Important

Do NOT write "repeat the process" in your explanation. Explain every step, every time.
