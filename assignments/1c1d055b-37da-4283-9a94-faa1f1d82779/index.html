Your Mission

Design a keychain with your name in Fusion 360. Follow the video to build it, then choose 2 colors so it can be 3D printed.

You will learn · 01

Text & Extrude

Write your name with the Text tool and turn it into 3D letters.

You will learn · 02

Offset

Draw a base around your letters so they hold together as one piece.

You will learn · 03

Ring & Fillet

Add a ring for your keys and smooth the corners where it joins the base.

Before You Start
  • Your name or nickname, 5 letters max.
  • A thick font, like Arial Black or Impact, so your name is easy to see.
  • Thin or handwriting fonts. Thin parts break or don't print.
  • One piece: every letter touches the base.

Part 01 · Build the Keychain

Watch the video and build your keychain in Fusion 360 at the same time. Pause it whenever you need to.

0:00 / 0:00
  1. Set your units to millimeters, like the video shows.
  2. Follow the video, but type your name instead of "Hello".
  3. Choose a thick font from the Font list.
  4. Check that every letter sits on the base. The end of the video shows how to connect a letter that doesn't touch.
  5. Save your design as Lastname_Keychain.
Part 02 · Pick 2 Colors

Your keychain prints in 2 colors: one for the base and ring, one for your letters. Pick colors that stand out from each other.

5 letters max.
